about me

Introduction

Hey Everyone! 
My name is Sam Kingpin, and I built this website out of pure passion. Over the past few years, I saw a huge problem, I couldn't unsee. the PC building community has become increasingly toxic toward beginners. It feels like every hardware choice you make gets instantly criticized, ignoring the fact of whether you're right or wrong. That’s exactly why I created this website to publish my work "The Absolute No-Shit Guide to PCs." This book is made to teach you all the unwritten rules, nuances, and hardware concepts the industry somehow expects you to magically know. Unlike traditional guides who wanna be academic or overly complicated textbooks, I wrote this so anyone can pick it up in the simplest, most human way possible.

What's Next?

While the book is the main focus right now, I plan on expanding this site way beyond PC hardware. Expect future materials covering
Robotics Simulation: Gazebo, Webots & NVIDIA Isaac Sim
Robotics Middleware: ROS2
Hardware Protocols: CAN Bus, UART, I2C & SPI
Embedded Software: C / C++ Systems Programming
Control & Math: MATLAB Simulations
Hardware Design: Parametric CAD (Fusion 360, though I might post it as a video)
PCB Design : KiCAD 
And in v2.0 that i plan to release next year, I will be adding features like Daily Tech News and other cool features.
